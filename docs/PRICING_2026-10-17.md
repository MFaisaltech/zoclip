## Accounting and limits

Existing duration pricing remains: maximum of 60 minimum job credits and
`ceil(source_seconds / 60) * 5 + output_count * 2 + edit_count * 2`;
captions/reframing currently add zero. Existing per-job, daily/monthly, concurrency,
provider admission and aggregate spending controls remain authoritative.
The quote must match the actual validated source duration; request payloads cannot
substitute an advertised clip target for the quoted credits.

Examples for one output: a one-minute video costs 60 credits, 15 minutes costs
77, and 120 minutes costs 602. Starter's 500 credits cannot reserve the last job.
Paid source limit remains 120 minutes and current per-job output/edit limits are
unchanged. Targets do not bypass those limits or guarantee a specific result count.

Free terms configure a dormant 60-credit allowance, enough for exactly one quoted
clip from a source up to five minutes. Installing terms grants nothing. Existing
once-only entitlement grants remain unique across versions. Additionally, quotes
and reservations count all held/settled free-plan output slots across historical
price versions while holding the PostgreSQL wallet lock. Extra balance, concurrent
requests, price-version changes and new sessions cannot reset the lifetime clip.
Proven final failure can release a reservation; existing expense controls still
apply before any later attempt. Automatic trial enablement remains rejected.

New purchases snapshot PKR 29,900 / 69,900 / 129,900 paisa and 500 / 1,300 / 2,400
credits. Only verified payment evidence grants those credits, once. Existing paid
orders/entitlements keep their original snapshots. Business/top-up sale terms were
retired with new non-purchasable versions; historical rows and access were preserved.
The paid packages retain their prior unapproved commercial gate, 30-day prepaid
access and disabled credit expiry/automatic renewal settings.
